Featured project

Fontanella

My first published iOS app. It is not an AI project, but it shows my ability to build, ship, and maintain a real product from idea to App Store release.

Find free drinking water nearby.

Fontanella uses map and location features to show nearby public fountains, walking distance, favorites, and navigation through Apple Maps or Google Maps. It currently supports Roma, Milano, Torino, Napoli, and Firenze.
